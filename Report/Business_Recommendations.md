# Business Analysis Summary

## Key Insights
- Completed orders generated total sales of ₹82,456,306.09.
- Estimated total profit for completed orders is ₹6,544,153.96.
- The state with the highest sales is TX at ₹20,635,847.22.
- The state with the lowest sales is DC at ₹4,011,796.05.
- Electronics is the category with the highest revenue.
- Using Brand as a proxy for sub-category, KiddoFun is the top group by sales.
- The best month for sales is May 2024.
- The most common payment method is Credit Card.
- Some strong-selling categories have lower profit margins, so pricing and cost control should be reviewed there.

## Recommendations
1. Invest more in the states with the best performance and run local campaigns in the weaker states.
2. Continue supporting top-selling categories while reviewing margins in lower-profit areas.
3. Promote popular payment methods and use busy months to introduce sales promotions.
